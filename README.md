## About the Project

This AI-System Analyzer is intended to be a useful tool for people who may not have extensive technical knowledge. 
Many users don't have the expertise to troubleshoot problems with their machines or know where to begin researching an issue.

The analyzer is split into three main parts:

1. Python System Scanner: A Python script scans the connected machine and collects system information, hardware metrics, running processes, services, and other relevant data.

2. AI Analysis: The collected data is analyzed by AI agents that look for potential issues, identify possible causes, and provide troubleshooting recommendations. They can also help connect detected issues with relevant documentation.

3. Centralized Dashboard: A web-based interface displays the collected system information, detected issues, and recommended solutions in an organized and easy-to-understand format.

The overall goal is to make system monitoring and troubleshooting more accessible to everyday users while reducing the time and effort needed to identify and understand technical problems.










Problem-Solving Approach

1. Problem Identified

The main problem I am trying to solve is one that I encountered heavily when I began my tech journey, computers can often throw errors for unknown reasons. Driver errors, blue screens with complex error codes, hardware issues, etc.
And before the age of AI, researching these problems often led you to forum posts and old tech articles to find a fix. Which can be difficult to navigate for those who are not literate in technology.
I wanted to make the task of identifying and fixing tech-related problems much simpler for the average user.

2. My Approach

First, I knew I wanted to keep the analyzer as simple as possible while also being mutable, having the ability to change processes and outcomes allows for flexibility depending on the user.
I also wanted the analyzer to work with any type of computer I feed it, no matter the Operating System or hardware in it. Using 2 different AI agents that communicate with each other, the data that is collected
can flow one way and pass multiple points of analysis and documentation.

3. Challenges & Solutions

One of the main challenges was figuring out how to collect useful system information and organize it in a way that AI agents could understand and analyze. 
Another challenge was connecting the different components so they could communicate and work together.

4. Results

The project brings system scanning, AI-assisted troubleshooting, and system information together in one interface. It helps users better understand their machines, recognize potential issues, and find possible solutions.

5. Future Improvements

Future improvements include expanding support for different operating systems, improving the accuracy of AI-generated recommendations, and introducing additional troubleshooting and automated repair capabilities.
